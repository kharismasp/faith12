# Faith · Mirrors & Minutes — Fixed Warm-ups & Blank Working Space

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. The site also works offline, with no build, CDN, external font or account required. Some browsers restrict localStorage on file:// URLs; lessons still run, but records may not persist. Offline and hosted copies have separate browser records.

## Revised student structure

Both sessions now have seven sections:

1. Jump garden — exactly 4 multiplication questions.
2. Mirror match — exactly 3 equivalent-fraction questions.
3. Fraction workshop — exactly 3 operations: 2 additions, then 1 subtraction.
4. Rest stop — manual resume.
5. Session 1: Clock steps. Session 2: Remember & try.
6. Session 1: One whole day. Session 2: Time bridge.
7. Little snapshot — 3 exit questions.

The former “My own turn” transfer section has been removed.

## A single forward button

There is one forward button at the bottom of a question screen. It remains disabled until the current answer is correct. On intermediate questions it advances only to the next question, with an explicit count. On the last question it changes to the next section after success. The bank does not loop. There is no separate in-card Next question plus a competing Continue button.

The same forward-button pattern applies to time quizzes, recall, elapsed-time questions and exit snapshots. The top navigation and lesson map remain available to the teacher for intentional section changes. Exploration screens can still move to the next section without answering a question. The rest stop has a direct resume button.

## Gradual warm-up banks

Multiplication is written here as number of groups × group size.

| Set | Session 1 | Session 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Multiplication | 2×2; 3×2; 3×3; 4×3 | 3×2; 3×3; 4×3; 5×3 |
| Equivalent fractions | 1/2 = ?/4; 2/3 = ?/6; 3/5 = ?/10 | 1/3 = ?/6; 2/5 = ?/10; 3/4 = ?/12 |
| Addition 1 | 1/2 + 1/4 | 1/3 + 1/6 |
| Addition 2 | 2/3 + 1/6 | 2/5 + 3/10 |
| Subtraction | 3/4 − 1/8 | 3/4 − 1/6 |

The multiplication warm-up has no clue, picture-hint, or worked-example buttons. The main jump garden and equal-group activity remain available. Students are invited to say or write how they are counting before answering.

The multiplication warm-up moves from familiar 2s into repeated practice of 3s. The fraction operation common denominators rise through 4, 6, 8 in session 1 and 6, 10, 12 in session 2. The last session-2 subtraction needs both fractions renamed. All results stay between zero and one; equivalent unsimplified final answers are accepted.

## Your thinking space

Equivalent-fraction and fraction-operation questions have a blank white working area next to the question, under “Your thinking space”. Students use Zoom’s annotation pen to write their method. The site adds no drawing tools, fraction bars, grid, model, or prefilled steps to this area. Zoom manages its annotations.

Extra clues, on-demand pictures and different examples remain available separately from the blank working space. Answer fields and question progression are unchanged.

## Teacher notes and records

Teacher notes are available from the header, in Indonesian. Records show check attempts, whether an item has ever been answered correctly, and the highest extra support used. “No extra help” means that no additional site hint was used; it does not capture oral teacher assistance or Zoom annotations.

Warm-up questions changed in this edition, so their earlier answer records are not reused. Unchanged time and snapshot records migrate from the previous edition. Records remain browser-local and separate for the two sessions. Choosing a session does not clear its records. Reset asks for confirmation and clears only the selected session. Exact page and answer fields are not restored after navigation/reload. Zoom annotations are managed outside the site.

## Teaching use

Ask for a prediction before each multiplication jump. Connect jumps with repeated addition and equal groups. Let the child write her own calculation steps in the blank space using Zoom annotations, then enter the answer. Offer additional hints only when needed. After support, start the next question with extra hints closed again. The top map permits a deliberate teacher-led break or change of section if needed.

The two-session time content remains: five-minute clock steps, linked 12-/24-hour formats, explicit noon/midnight, retrieval practice, and optional elapsed-time jumps through whole hours. Crossing midnight is an optional stretch. No countdown, loss of hearts, or focus penalties.

This is a teaching aid adapted from the supplied site and requested objectives, not an official Cambridge resource or a validated intervention. Artwork is retained from the supplied archive. Mathematical bars, clocks, equal groups and timelines are HTML/SVG.
